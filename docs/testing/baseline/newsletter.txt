  Community Success Stories

   Carlos' Journey to College
   Carlos, a participant in our mentorship program, has been accepted to the University of California! Coming
   from a low-income household, Carlos faced many challenges in pursuing higher education, but with the
   guidance of his mentor and the support of our college readiness workshops, he achieved his dream. "I never
   thought college was a real possibility for me," says Carlos. "But with the help of this program, I learned how to
   apply and even received scholarships. I'm so excited for the future."
   We are so proud of Carlos and all the students who continue to push boundaries and strive for excellence.
